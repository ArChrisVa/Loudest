====================

  Licence text: resources\app.asar.unpacked\node_modules\ffmpeg-static\ffmpeg.exe.LICENSE
  Build notes:  resources\app.asar.unpacked\node_modules\ffmpeg-static\ffmpeg.exe.README
  Source code:  https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg/commit/e38092ef93
                https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/
  Packaged by:  ffmpeg-static 5.3.0, https://github.com/eugeneware/ffmpeg-static

Electron 45 (includes Chromium and Node.js)
  The window, the video player and the app's own code run on it.
  Licence:      MIT, with many parts under their own licences
  Licence text: LICENSE.electron.txt and LICENSES.chromium.html, beside Loudest.exe
  Source code:  https://github.com/electron/electron

ONNX Runtime 1.31 (onnxruntime-node)
  Runs the sound-recognition model.
  Licence:      MIT, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation
  Licence text: https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE
  Source code:  https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

YAMNet
  The sound-recognition model that hears laughs, screams, shouts, speech and
  music. Made by Google (TensorFlow Model Garden, AudioSet), used here in the
  ONNX form published at https://huggingface.co/audiomagic/yamnet-onnx
  Licence:      Apache License 2.0
  Licence text: resources\app.asar.unpacked\models\yamnet-LICENSE.txt
  Source:       https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/audioset/yamnet


FETCHED WHEN FIRST NEEDED
=========================

These are not part of the installer. Loudest downloads each one from its
makers' own page the first time the feature that needs it is used, and keeps
it in its data folder (%APPDATA%\loudest\tools).

yt-dlp
  Downloads the chat of a YouTube stream ("Get chat" with a YouTube link).
  Licence:      The Unlicense; its Windows program also contains parts under
                the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later
  Home:         https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp

TwitchDownloaderCLI
  Downloads the chat of a Twitch stream ("Get chat" with a Twitch link).
  Licence:      MIT, Copyright (c) lay295 and contributors
  Home:         https://github.com/lay295/TwitchDownloader
